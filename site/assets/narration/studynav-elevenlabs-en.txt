StudyNav adds notes, precise links, short downloads, and extra player controls to supported JW.org pages in Chromium browsers.

Highlight the words you need in one of six colors. Add a note or tags, and keep the note beside the text when the window is wide enough.

Press Save place beside a paragraph or verse. Later, open the StudyNav library and return directly to that saved place.

Select the text and copy it with the publication title and a link. The result is ready to paste into a message or notes.

Show a QR code for the current page or selected verse, then scan it with your phone.

StudyNav opens the same publication through a JW.org address without the extra technical parts. The shorter link is easier to save or send.

Select one verse or several consecutive verses. Press Download audio, and StudyNav saves the selected narration as one WAV file.

The copied result keeps only the verse words, without its number, reference letters, footnotes, or StudyNav buttons.

Select the first and last verse. The Link button copies one address that opens the whole selected range.

If you know a publication code or document number, press Control or Command plus Shift and K. This opens the regular JW.org and WOL search, not an AI search.

Open the caption and text description that the page authors provided for the image.

After you turn this feature on, a regular Download image button appears below suitable images in an article.

Beside the language control, StudyNav shows the exact number of translations available for the current page.

The article title and navigation buttons stay at the top of the window, so you do not need to return to the beginning of a long page.

On a wide screen, the article uses more space and fits more words on each line. Study panels and dialogs do not stretch.

Article tables gain spacing, borders, and alternating row backgrounds, making it easier to follow each row.

When you move the pointer over a video and the player buttons appear, the picture no longer darkens around the edges.

Player captions become larger and higher-contrast, making them easier to read.

Space or K pauses and resumes the video. J and L seek, M mutes, and F enters full screen.

StudyNav copies the page address with the current time. The recipient can see where to seek, but the link itself does not start at that second.

Enter the start and end of the part you need. You can save up to two minutes of audio as WAV or one minute of video as WebM.

StudyNav remembers where you stopped the video. Next time, press Resume to return to that point without automatic playback.

The Second display button opens the video or audio in a small separate window. It does not download the media file.

If the media has captions or a prepared transcript, a button appears to search and download the text. If no text is available, the button stays hidden.

Download the StudyNav archive, unzip it, and load the folder from the extensions page in your Chromium browser.
