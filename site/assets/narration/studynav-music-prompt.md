# Eleven Music setup — StudyNav tutorial background

Use Eleven Music v2. Set the duration to **4:40** and copy each part into its
matching field.

## Prompt

Create a 4 minute 40 second instrumental-only background track for a clear
software tutorial. Light, clean, ethereal guitar ambient with an even energy
level from beginning to end. Use a delicate clean electric guitar playing
occasional widely spaced harmonics, isolated soft notes, and faint open chords.
Let every short phrase decay naturally into clear silence. Keep the arrangement
thin, quiet, and unhurried, leaving plenty of space for spoken narration.
Neutral, unobtrusive, high-fidelity studio-quality mix with a short gentle
fade-in and fade-out.

## Include Styles

light ambient, ethereal clean guitar, delicate electric guitar harmonics,
widely spaced single notes, faint open chords, natural decay, silence between
phrases, minimal ambient, calm, neutral, unobtrusive, sparse arrangement, flat
dynamics, narration-friendly, high fidelity, clean mix, instrumental

## Exclude Styles

vocals, spoken word, choir, drums, percussion, beat, rhythmic pulse, metronome,
ticking, clicks, hi-hats, kick, snare, rhythmic ostinato, arpeggios, rhythmic
plucks, guitar strumming, fingerpicking pattern, rhythmic guitar, guitar solo,
lead guitar, bass throb, sub-bass pulse, sustained synth pads, humming, buzzing,
low drone, dark drone, continuous drone, evolving drone, swelling pads, rising
layers, build-up, dramatic swells, crescendos, tension, dominant melody, catchy
hook, repetitive loop, wall of sound, abrupt transitions, risers, impacts,
sound effects, noise, hiss, crackle, vinyl noise, feedback, distortion, harsh
frequencies, excessive reverb, excessive shimmer

Generate two versions. Choose the one that stays behind the voice without
creating a beat or pulling attention toward the music.
