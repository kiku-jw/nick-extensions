Dark mode that adapts to the page.

InkShade transforms website colors locally while preserving layouts, media, forms, CSS variables, dynamic content, and Shadow DOM. Its rendered-palette detector avoids applying another theme when a page is already genuinely dark.

Full-page rendering

A pinned dynamic theme engine handles ordinary pages, complex stylesheets, frames, and content added after load instead of flattening every site with one blanket stylesheet.

Native-dark awareness

InkShade evaluates the rendered viewport and a bounded sample of long documents. A dark hero alone does not hide a light page, while confirmed native-dark pages stay untouched.

Local by design

No account, advertising, telemetry, news feed, premium activation, or remote configuration. Preferences and site rules remain in browser extension storage unless you explicitly export them.

Controls that stay yours

Adjust brightness, contrast, sepia, fonts, automation, and per-site behavior. Disable another dark-mode extension on the same page to avoid competing theme engines.

Independent project: InkShade is built from a reviewed, pinned revision of the MIT-licensed Dark Reader engine. InkShade has its own identity and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Dark Reader Ltd. Website compatibility varies, so fixes remain narrow and reviewable rather than claiming perfection on every site.
